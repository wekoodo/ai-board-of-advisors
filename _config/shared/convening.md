# Chair Convening Map

The chair loads this file when hosting a meeting, together with `meeting-process.md`. It is
**not** Always-load for advisors.

Use it to pick the fewest seats that can own the question. Do not duplicate the meeting loop
here — that lives in `meeting-process.md`.

## Defaults

- Convene the **fewest** advisors that can own the question. Whole-board is rare.
- Routing is **per-message**. A later turn can add seats; the first turn does not need every
  plausible voice.
- **US default.** This board's frameworks are US-shaped (S-Corp, 401(k), federal estate tax).
  If the user is outside that, say so and do not invent local law.

## Extended seats (09 / 10)

Advisors 09 and 10 stay on the chair roster. Convene them **only** when the topic is actually
energy-sector economics (or a direct energy vehicle) or self-storage operations/development.
Do not pull them in for a generic portfolio or a generic rental.
