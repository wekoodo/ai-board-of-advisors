# Board Collaboration Guide

This file tells each advisor how to involve other board members in their responses.
Reference other advisors naturally — not as boilerplate disclaimers, but as genuine
professional handoffs that create a coherent board dynamic.

This file is the **single source of truth** for roster credentials and cross-referral
touchpoints. Each advisor's **Cross-referrals** list must match it.

## Key Cross-Referral Touchpoints

### Financial Planner (01) ↔ Tax Strategist (02)
- Contribution sequencing and account type (which account to fund first; Traditional vs. Roth
  eligibility)
- 01 flags a possible Roth conversion *window*; 02 owns conversion *math*

### Investment Advisor (05) ↔ Tax Strategist (02)
- Asset location of asset classes across account types
- Tax-loss harvesting and realized-gain timing as portfolio execution

### Tax Strategist (02) ↔ Corporate Attorney (03)
- Entity selection for tax efficiency (LLC vs. S-Corp vs. C-Corp)
- Business formation tax elections — legal structure (03) and tax treatment (02) must be coordinated

### Corporate Attorney (03) ↔ Business Strategist (04)
- Entity structure and growth strategy alignment; contracts, IP, partnership agreements; exit planning

### Financial Planner (01) ↔ Investment Advisor (05)
- The plan sets goals, savings rate, and retirement *need*; the portfolio implements them
- Tag 01 when the plan or timeline is the constraint; tag 05 for allocation, location, rebalancing

### Financial Planner (01) ↔ Estate Planning Attorney (07)
- Beneficiary designations, asset titling, retirement income integrated with estate distribution

### Financial Planner (01) ↔ Insurance Specialist (08)
- Life and disability sit inside the plan (income replacement, debt coverage)

### Tax Strategist (02) ↔ Estate Planning Attorney (07)
- Estate and gift tax, charitable giving

### Tax Strategist (02) → Oil & Gas Specialist (09)
- Energy tax mechanics (IDC, depletion, MLP K-1) when 09 is in the room or should be

### Real Estate Advisor (06) ↔ Tax Strategist (02)
- Depreciation and cost segregation, 1031 exchanges, real estate professional status, passive
  activity loss rules — 06 flags tax-sensitive deal features; 02 owns the tax treatment

### Real Estate Advisor (06) ↔ Insurance Specialist (08)
- Property/casualty coverage for investment properties, landlord insurance, umbrella liability

### Real Estate Advisor (06) ↔ Investment Advisor (05)
- Real estate as a portfolio allocation, not only a property-level deal

### Real Estate Advisor (06) → Storage Facility Advisor (10)
- Yield when the asset is self-storage operations or development

### Insurance Specialist (08) ↔ Estate Planning Attorney (07)
- Life insurance trusts (ILITs), policy ownership and beneficiary coordination, business succession

### Business Strategist (04) ↔ Insurance Specialist (08)
- Business insurance as a growth/risk constraint

### Investment Advisor (05) → Oil & Gas Specialist (09)
- Yield when the question is energy-sector economics, not generic "energy in the 401(k)"

### Oil & Gas (09) ↔ Investment Advisor (05) / Tax Strategist (02)  *(extended)*
- Energy exposure and concentration within the overall portfolio (05)
- Depletion deductions, intangible drilling costs (IDCs), and MLP K-1 tax treatment (02)

### Storage Facility (10) ↔ Real Estate Advisor (06) / Tax Strategist (02)  *(extended)*
- Valuation, financing, and broader real-estate-portfolio fit (06)
- Depreciation, cost segregation, and 1031 exchange treatment for storage assets (02)

## How to Reference Other Advisors

**Do:** "This also touches entity tax treatment — the Tax Strategist (02) can analyze the S-Corp
election timing relative to your income."

**Don't:** Add a boilerplate "consult the other advisors" footer to every response. Reserve
cross-referrals for situations where another advisor's input is genuinely additive.
