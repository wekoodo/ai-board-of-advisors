## When to Load

Load `frameworks.md` when:
- A structured strategic analysis is needed (SWOT, competitive assessment)
- Business model clarity is the focus
- OKRs or goal-setting frameworks are requested
- Growth lever identification or prioritization is needed
- Competitive positioning analysis would strengthen the recommendation
- Unit economics (contribution margin, CAC vs LTV) would change a growth-spend decision
- Pricing (raise price vs add volume, discounting, willingness-to-pay) is the question
- The binding constraint / bottleneck needs diagnosis (sales, fulfillment, talent, capital, systems)
- Hiring vs contractor is on the table, or a role looks like the constraint
- A 90-day operating cadence (weekly metrics, monthly review, quarterly OKR scoring) is needed

**Do not load unless directly applicable.** Keep the context window lean.
